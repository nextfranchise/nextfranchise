Module 1: FOH Standards 1.2 Grooming & Personal Appearance

"Your uniform is a promise of quality. Before you serve a single guest, you have to look the part."

Step 1: Suit Up

✓

Clean & Complete: Arrive in the full issued uniform. No missing aprons or name tags. Laundry is your responsibility—arrive crisp.

✓

The Posture: Stand tall. Slouching signals boredom to our guests. An upright posture signals you are ready to serve.

MISSING PIECES? Check the branch "Spare Stock" for emergency replacements. You cannot start service without being 100% compliant.

Step 2: Hygiene & Vibe

✓

The Hand Standard: Fingernails must be clean and trimmed. Guests watch your hands more than your face during every transaction.

✓

Hair & Scent: Hair must be neat and tied back. Avoid strong perfumes—they clash with the fresh aroma of our food.

KPI TARGET: 100% Pre-Shift Grooming Pass Rate. Every Crew Member must pass a visual check by the MOD before service.
