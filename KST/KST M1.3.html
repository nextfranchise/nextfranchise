Module 1: FOH Standards 1.3 Opening Procedure

"Success is built in the first 30 minutes. If we win the morning prep, we win the whole day."

PHASE 1

Step 1: The Daily Walkthrough

Arrive early. Before you touch a guest order, you must ensure the "Stage" is ready. Walk the dining area and scan for hazards or debris.

Key Tasks:

  • Unlock premises and check safety lighting.
  • Complete your handwashing and grooming check.
  • Join the MOD for the daily briefing.
WHY IT MATTERS: A late start ripples through the whole shift. On-time opening is verified by your POS transaction logs.
PHASE 2

Step 2: Power Up

Once the environment is safe, bring the technology to life. Every piece of FOH equipment must be powered and checked.

The Technical Checklist:

  • Power on POS terminals and food warmers.
  • Verify all service equipment is functioning.
  • Sign off every line item on the Opening Checklist.
KPI TARGET: 100% Checklist Completion Rate. Do not sign a line item until the task is physically finished.
