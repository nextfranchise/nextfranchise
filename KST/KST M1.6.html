Module 1: FOH Standards 1.6 End-of-Shift Reporting

"A great shift doesn't end when the door locks. It ends when the next team knows exactly what they're walking into."

Step 1: Recount & Record

Log the Legacy

Before you leave, perform a mental replay. Record low stock, equipment glitches, or notable guest feedback. Be factual and specific.

📎 [TOOL_001] Shift Report Log — Available in LMS
AVOID VAGUENESS: Entries like "All good" are unhelpful. If we're down to 2 bags of rice, write: "Inventory: Rice low (2 bags remaining)."
Step 2: The Briefing

Don't Just Log It

If an issue is urgent (safety risk, broken lock, POS failure), you must speak to the Manager on Duty (MOD) before you walk out.

  • Verbally flag anything that can't wait for the next shift.
  • Hand off or leave the log in the designated area.
KPI TARGET: 100% Urgent Issue Escalation. Never leave a safety or security concern for the next team to "find."
