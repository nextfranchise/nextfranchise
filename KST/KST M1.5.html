Module 1: FOH Standards 1.5 FOH-BOH Communication

"The POS sends the data, but your communication sends the quality. Let's make sure the front and back speak the same language."

ACCURACY TARGET: 100%

Step 1: Precise Entry

✓

Double-Check the Guest: Read the order summary back to the guest before hitting "Send." One correction at the counter saves 10 minutes in the kitchen.

✓

The Verbal Bridge: If there's a modification (no onions, extra sauce) that isn't a standard button, you must verbally flag it to the BOH Cook.

RUSH RULE: During peak hours, rely primarily on the POS ticket. Use verbal talk only for exceptions to keep the kitchen noise down.
SPEED TARGET: BRANCH-TARGET

Step 2: Verification

✓

Monitor the Clock: Keep an eye on ticket times. If an order is running behind the branch target, sync with the BOH Cook immediately to re-prioritize.

✓

The Final Match: Before the food touches the guest's hand, compare the plate to the ticket. If it doesn't match 100%, send it back for correction before the guest sees it.

TEAM SYNC: Discrepancies should be flagged calmly and immediately. We fix it as a team, not as rivals.
