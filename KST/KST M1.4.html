Module 1: FOH Standards 1.4 Closing Procedure

"A successful close protects our equipment, our money, and our team. Let's finish as strong as we started."

PHASE 1: THE RESET

Step 1: Clean & Secure

✓

The Cut-off: Stop accepting new guests exactly at closing time. Complete service for those already inside.

✓

Put Gear to Sleep: Power down all POS terminals and food warmers per manufacturer guidance. Leaving them on is a fire hazard and wastes energy.

CASHIER SYNC: Confirm the Cashier Closing Procedure (Module 2.2) is signed off before you move to final lockdown.
PHASE 2: LOCKDOWN

Step 2: The Final Sign-off

✓

The Perimeter: Lock all doors, windows, and access points. Physically shake the handles—don't just look at them.

✓

The Hand-off: Sign every line of the Closing Checklist and hand it directly to the MOD. We never leave until the MOD clears the walkthrough.

KPI TARGET: 100% Security Pass Rate. A single unlocked door is a critical safety failure.
