Module 1: FOH Standards 1.1 Guest Greeting & Farewell

"Greeting a guest is the first 'bite' of their K-Street experience. Let's make it hit the mark."

Step 1: Immediate Connection

The moment a guest enters, the clock starts. You have five seconds to acknowledge them. Even if your hands are full, eye contact and a warm smile meet the K-Street standard.

[SCRIPT] "Annyeong, Welcome to K-Street!"
KPI ALERT: Mystery shoppers verify these specific phrases. Consistent delivery is the target for 100% of guests.

Step 2: The Lasting Impression

The service isn't over when the guest pays. As they head toward the door, pause, make eye contact again, and send them off with energy.

[SCRIPT] "Kamsahamnida, See us Again!"

Pro-Tip: Never let a guest leave "to the back of your head." Turn around and finish the interaction properly.
