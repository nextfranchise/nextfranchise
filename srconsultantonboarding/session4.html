The Downstream Cascade

The investor pitch deck, the Franchise Agreement clauses, and the sales qualification script are built strictly from the Blueprint. A weak card becomes a weak slide, a weak legal clause, and a false promise to a family's life savings.

The Trace Test Crucible:

Pick any single number or claim in a client's pitch deck. Trace it backward to its Blueprint card, and from the card to its verified source in Appendix A or the Financial Goals workbook. If the trail breaks anywhere, the Blueprint is disqualified.

DISCOVERY 02 STAGE PACE: 10 MINUTES

The Blueprint in One Sentence

Written Last · Placed First
The 3-Part Structural Formula
Formula Breakdown:

1. The Market Gap: What does the territory lack that customers crave?

2. The Unit Proof: Exactly how much gross sales/volume do existing units prove?

3. The Franchisee Gain: What concrete life outcome does this deliver to a local owner?

Snaxxville Benchmark Sentence:

"Most towns in Northern Samar have no branded snack or fast-food outlet, and Snaxxville—already selling ₱726k–₱862k a month in two of them—is the only local brand ready to franchise into the rest."

"For a franchisee from the province, that means a chance to own a proven business in their own town, instead of risking life savings on an untested stall."

Could a competitor's name be swapped in without changing a word? If yes, it fails.
THE HONESTY CASE: K-STREET

Naming the Failure it Solves

K-Street Korean Food Counters:

"Thirty of sixty previous franchises failed because they relied on mall foot-traffic rather than occupancy cost limits. The new K-Street Blueprint rebuilds expansion around strict rent-to-sales ratios and audited commissary delivery."

The Weak Consultant Version (Disqualified):

"A fast-growing food brand with delicious products and a passionate team, ready to expand nationwide."

Why it fails: Zero gap named. Zero proof numbers. Zero franchisee stakes.
Governs pitch deck Slide 1.

Part 1: The Customer // Cards 1.1 to 1.4

Customer Obsession · Ground Truth · First Principles
The 4 Cards of Part 1 THE DINER / USER
1.1 Power Customer (First Person) BEHAVIORAL

"I'm a young worker earning around ₱440 a day in a town with no mall. You'll recognize me because I arrive after 3:00 PM with two coworkers to share merienda and unwind."

Separates payer from user (e.g. at Teamworks, mother pays, child receives therapy).
1.2 Power Substitutes (Sets Price Ceiling) BEHAVIOR > BRANDS

Primary substitute is homemade merienda or street carts outside school (fishball/banana cue). Rain is a substitute in itself (keeps people home), which justifies Facebook-page delivery.

1.3 Territory Rule & 1.4 Market Evidence PSA CENSUS BACKED

Penetration = Weekly Transactions ÷ Catchment Population. Snaxxville Catubig achieves 2.3% weekly penetration. Palapag has more people, but 1/5th the penetration because the site was on a deserted road.

INTERACTIVE TERRITORY ENGINE

Solve Minimum Town Population

To achieve Conservative volume (100 txns/day = 700 txns/week), how large must the town catchment segment be at a conservative 3% weekly penetration?

Weekly Target Txns: 700 txns / week
Conservative Penetration: 3.0%
700 ÷ 0.03 = 23,333 People
Snaxxville Territory Rule: Towns of 23,000+ residents qualify for Phase 1.
The Demand Score Rule:

Territory demand score is rated out of 10 based on actual customer behavior (repeat purchase, unprompted orders). Below 7/10 means the territory rule is invalid.

Part 2: The Brand // Proof & Turnaround Story

Radical Candor · Include Weak Branches
Cards 2.1 & 2.2 // The Cost & The Boundary
2.1 Founder Story (The Turnaround Lesson):

Two years ago, Laoang branch sales fell to single digits. Instead of closing, the founders moved to a larger high-visibility site, added dine-in seating, and updated the combo menu. Today, it averages 116 daily transactions.

"That turnaround is the lesson behind the franchise system: the right site format matters more than the gross population of the town."
2.2 The Business:

Defines the exact operational boundaries: counter service, dine-in, and delivery through Facebook page (10–15% of revenue). Lists all required FDA/LGU health permits and proprietary recipes.

Franchisees buy the founder's battle-tested judgment, not generic corporate enthusiasm.

Part 3A: The Franchisee // Cards 3.1 to 3.4

Customer Obsession · Whole Person · Systems Thinking
Cards 3.1, 3.2 & 3.3 // Persona, Outcome & Alternatives
3.1 Power Franchisee (First Person):

"I'm from this province. I work abroad and plan to return, or I run a local shop, and I've saved ₱1.5M. You'll recognize me because I ask how many months until I recover my cash before I even ask about the menu."

3.2 The 4-Box Outcome // The Psycho-Spiritual Driver:
Direct: Steady cash flow
Social: Respected business owner
Financial: Recoup ₱1.58M in 13 mos
Psycho-Spiritual: Never go abroad again
3.3 Power Alternatives: The real competitor is not another food franchise—it is keeping savings in the bank, buying a jeepney, or building a retirement home.
"Wants to avoid losing savings that took 10 years abroad to build is the sales hook."
Card 3.4 Power Pain DNA // Causal Chains NO FRICTION LISTS
The Weak Version (Banned):

"Pains: Competition, rising ingredient costs, finding staff, bad location, marketing."

Why it fails: 5 disconnected symptoms. Zero systemic causality.
The True Causal Chain (The White Bucket / K-Street):
1. Working capital underestimated in Mode 1
↓ 2. Staff hours cut to conserve payroll
↓ 3. Service slips; regulars drift away
↓ 4. Sales drop below Conservative; more hours cut
↓ 5. Unit collapses in Month 14
Systems Thinking Rule: Find the link that keeps the loop repeating. Attack that link.

DISCOVERY 06 STAGE PACE: 40 MINUTES

Cards 3.5 to 3.10 // Solution, Readiness & Economics

Follow the Good · Relentless Execution · First Principles
Cards 3.5, 3.6 & 3.7 // Solution, Delta & Readiness
3.5 Power Solution DNA:

Every pain in 3.4 must be answered by a named system deliverable. At Teamworks: "Parent waits months for diagnosis" is answered by an HQ Program Manager evaluating every client monthly.

3.6 Delta 10X (Measurable Transformation):

Not generic slogans. From "Savings sitting idle in foreign bank" to "₱1,581,650 recovered in 13 months with family running daily counter."

3.7 Readiness Check // The "Not Yet" List:

Scores actual applicant actions (funds verified, branch visited, site found). Score below 7 = DO NOT SIGN. Explicitly rejects absentee investors and buyers borrowing money to afford the fee.

Readiness Check gives your brokerage team permission to say NO.
Cards 3.8, 3.9 & 3.10 // Usage & Economics
3.8 Power Usage // 5 Checkable Behaviors:
  1. Certify crew before store opening (contract gate).
  2. Report sales at 3:00 PM and closing daily.
  3. Maintain 14-day emergency buffer stock.
  4. Purchase proprietary mixes strictly from commissary.
  5. Owner attends weekly operations audit on-site.
3.9 & 3.10 Projections & Investment:

Every peso must match the approved Financial Goals workbook. Payback must be stated from signing against total cash before opening (Line 2.6).
