STAGE 04 OF 06 PAIR LAB · 01:20 – 01:40 (20 MINS)

The Radical Candor Protocol // "What's Weak Here"

Skill Anchor: Radical Candor (Level 4)
The 3 Structural Moves of Candor
MOVE 01 // State the Weak Point Plainly

No softening, no diplomatic preamble. "At Conservative volume, payback is 30 months, not 12."

MOVE 02 // Name Why It Hurts Capital

Connect weakness to real consequence. "If we sell 12 and a franchisee lives 30, they deplete their working capital in month 6."

MOVE 03 // Offer a Concrete Next Step

Give a viable path forward. "Show both Conservative and Target in 3.10, and require 6 months buffer capital."

The Defense Ban: The author may only respond with: "What I will change is [Action]." Any rationalizing or defending is an automatic disqualification.
Peer Review Crucible 8 MINS EACH
Live Review Protocol:
  1. Swap Session 1 homework drafts with your review partner.
  2. Reviewer writes exactly one paragraph titled "What's Weak Here".
  3. Reviewer reads it aloud looking into their partner's eyes.
  4. Partner responds only with: "What I will change is..."
Lead Facilitator Cue:

"Lead by example. Read an unflinching 'What's Weak Here' critique on your own recent work before the pairs begin."

Candor is an act of care, not cruelty.
