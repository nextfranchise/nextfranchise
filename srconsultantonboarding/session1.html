The Multiplier Stakes & Ground Rules

Stakes: Franchisee Life Savings
Systemic Risk

Why an Error in Franchising is Catastrophic

In normal consulting, an error harms a single corporate entity.

The Franchise Multiplier

In franchising, an untested assumption or flawed unit model is cloned across 10, 20, or 50 branches. Real families—OFWs, retirees, and life savings—bear the ultimate cost.

Live Probe // The 3-Sentence Crucible WHITEBOARD READY

"What is the single client problem you are stuck on right now? State it in 2 to 3 sentences in the customer's exact words—without proposing a solution yet."

The 6 Non-Negotiable Standards of Thought

Operating Foundation
01 Customer First

Start in the customer's and franchisee's life, never in the client's growth slide deck.

02 Their Words, Not Ours

Preserve customer and owner verbatim quotes. Taglish, emotional tone, and raw friction intact.

03 No Invented Facts

Any claim not directly seen, heard, or source-verified is marked Inferred until proven.

04 Say What's Weak

Critique of the work is never critique of the person. Uncomfortable truths protect capital.

05 Action-Proof Ending

No open-ended meetings. Every session closes with: One proof, one named owner, one date.

06 Strict Confidentiality

Client unit economics and store data never leave this room under any condition.

Trainees adhere to these 6 principles across all Blueprint deliverables and client reviews.

DISCOVERY 03 STAGE PACE: 25 MINUTES

The Mirror Test // Two Consulting Paradigms

Live Self-Audit
Decisive Moment The Traditional Consultant The NF Franchise Architect
01. Starting Point Takes the owner's pitch deck, menu, and growth ego targets ("open 50 branches in 3 years") as the assignment. Starts from the daily lives and fears of the end diner and future franchisee in their exact words.
02. The Numbers Transcribes sales targets, ROI claims, and payback numbers into glossy slides unchecked. Audits every figure: Heard, Inferred, Client-said, Confirmed. Builds strictly on Confirmed register data.
03. Branch Failures Treats branch complaints in isolation; prescribes local ad campaigns or ad-hoc menu changes. Draws the causal chain until it loops back, and destroys the root link keeping the failure cycle alive.
04. Strategic Advice Presents a laundry list of 20 unranked recommendations where everything is labeled critical. Power Law: Isolates one or two decisive priorities and forces an explicit "not yet" list signed by the owner.
05. Harsh Reality Softens data to keep the owner comfortable and safeguard monthly consulting retainers. Radical Candor: 3 moves (State the weak point, say why it hurts, offer a viable next step).
06. Meeting Exit Hands over a 60-page PDF report. Writes: "Next steps: TBD." Relentless Execution: leaves One Next Proof, One Named Owner, One Hard Date.
