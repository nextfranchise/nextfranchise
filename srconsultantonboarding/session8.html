The 4 Pushback Archetypes You Must Face LEAD IN ROLE

3. The Number Dismissal:

"Conservative volume of 100 transactions is ridiculous! My Catubig flagship does 120 easily. Let's raise Target."

4. The Ego Defense:

"I want 'Founders' Batch: Only 5 slots left' on the first slide! It works for other brands."

Passing Defense: Redirect to honest scarcity: 2 openings a month based on HQ training capacity.

THE ARCHITECT'S BALANCE

Stand Firm vs. Pivot Without Ego

When to Stand Firm:

Where verified register data, census stats, or unit break-even support your finding. Never cave to client ego.

When to Change:

Where the owner introduces a confirmed operational fact you had not verified. Pivot smoothly without defending.

Stubbornness is not strength. Grounded judgment is.
