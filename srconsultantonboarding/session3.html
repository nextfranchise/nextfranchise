The Financial Engine // Single Source of Truth

Core Architecture

The Workbook Wins. Always.

The Financial Goals workbook is created before the Blueprint. It is the mathematical spine behind the franchise agreement, pitch deck, and pro-forma.

The Source of Truth Rule

"When the Blueprint, sales deck, or contract disagree with the workbook, the workbook wins and everything else is corrected. No number enters without a named source."

Strict Workbook Color Codes:
Blue = Client / Source Input
Black = Dynamic Formula
Green = Cross-Tab Link
Yellow = Unconfirmed Estimate

The 4 Step Engineering Process

Iterative Protocol
1
Diagnose (Before Any Spreadsheet)

Audit locked inputs, critical gaps, red flags rated Green/Amber/Red, and Smart Fill defaults.

2
Iterate Tables by Script

Agree on Mode 1 and Mode 2 structures. Recalculate every figure mathematically—never typed by hand or eye.

3
Build & Link Formulas

Build tab-by-tab. Every formula links back to the Assumptions tab. Recalculate to confirm zero #REF! or loop errors.

4
Clarify & Version Control

Send the owner a concise clarification list. Replace yellow cells with blue client inputs. Raise version.

Ramp-Up // Payback From Signing vs Steady-State

Skill Anchor: Radical Candor & Follow the Good
Two Numbers, One Truth
Steady-State Payback
7.8 Mos

Calculated only after unit hits 120 txns/day.

Payback From Signing
13 Mos

Pre-opening fit-out + ramp climb included.

Why 13 Months is the Only Honest Metric:

A franchisee's life savings leave their bank account on the day the Franchise Agreement is signed—not on the day the counter rings its 120th transaction. Selling 7.8 months creates disillusionment and panic during pre-opening.

Ramp Climb Defaults: F&B: ~6 Mos | Services (Therapy/SPED): ~18 Mos
The Valley of Death // Deepest Loss CRITICAL DEFENSE

Who Absorbs the Pre-Break-Even Bleed?

A new unit loses cash in Month 1 and Month 2 during crew training and soft opening. The cumulative red ink before the first profitable month is the Deepest Cumulative Loss.

Mode 1 Line 2.6 Working Capital Requirement:

If the Deepest Cumulative Loss is ₱150,000, and your Mode 1 revolving fund only budgeted ₱80,000, the franchisee runs out of payroll cash in week seven.

Architect Gate:

The revolving fund in Mode 1 must 100% cover the deepest cumulative loss with a safety buffer.

OFFER GOVERNANCE

The Two Questions for Every Perk

1. Value vs. Payback Distinction:

A perk can have massive perceived value without moving payback by a single day. The prospect must hear both answers honestly.

2. Answer a Named Pain:

Every bonus must answer a specific sequence in Blueprint 3.4 Pain DNA. Bonuses that don't solve named pains erode franchisor margin without building loyalty.

The package price is sacred. Perks vary; the package stays fixed.
