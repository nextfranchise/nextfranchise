DISCOVERY 03 STAGE PACE: 90 MINUTES (30M PER CANDIDATE)

The 1-on-1 Scoring Calibration Protocol

Radical Candor in Action
The 3-Phase Calibration Breakdown
Phase 1: Candidate Self-Scores 10 Mins

Candidate presents self-ratings 1 to 4 with their single line of verified artifact evidence per skill.

Phase 2: Lead Reconciles on Evidence 15 Mins

Lead presents observed scores. Where ratings diverge, open the actual Blueprint cards, register audits, and field notes. Opinions are barred.

Phase 3: Agree on the 2 Focus Skills 5 Mins

Isolate the two lowest Senior Focus skills to anchor the candidate's next 90 days.

A trainee scored 4 without verified evidence learns nothing. Be direct.

DISCOVERY 05 OPERATING RHYTHM

Months 2 to 4 // Weekly & Monthly Routines

Proven Habits on Real Clients
The 4 Weekly Field Routines
1. Live Blueprint Ownership DAILY

Every client crisis goes on Template B1 Problem Card before reaching the owner.

2. Client Co-Lead & 15m Debrief SCHEDULED

Co-lead interview or review; immediate 15m debrief: what worked, what was weak, one change.

3. Peer Meta Skills Audit 45 MINS / WK

Bring one deliverable. Peers audit with 13-row check. Every Revise gets owner and date.

4. Reflection Log (Template B5) 15 MINS / WK

Pick 2 skills: 1 moment present, 1 moment missing. Shared strictly with lead consultant.

Weekly discipline prevents month-end panic.
Monthly Cadence & The Fall-Behind Law RE-SCORING
Monthly Milestones (Months 2, 3, 4):

Learning Dividend Session (30m): Group shares lessons learned on clients; best added to case bank.

Rubric Re-Scoring (30m): Re-score all 13 skills against new artifacts; adjust the 2 focus skills.

Gate Progress Tracking: Formally sign off gate items that now carry verified evidence.

The Fall-Behind Protocol:

If a trainee shows no progress on focus skills at monthly scoring: Name it plainly. Find the cause (time, client load, skill gap, motivation). If client workload is the blocker, management immediately reduces client volume. Training only works if there is time to practice.

Promotion comes when evidence is verified, never on a calendar date.

DISCOVERY 06 FINAL MASTER GATE

The Senior Consultant Certification Gate

All 6 Must Be True

Level 4 on 5 of 7 Focus Skills

Achieve Level 4 on at least 5 of the 7 Sr focus skills; Level 3+ on all others. Validated on Appendix C score sheet with verified evidence links.

GATE 02 TEMPLATE B1

3 Problem Cards Accepted

At least 3 complete Problem Cards accepted into Blueprint Part 5 Advisory across at least two live brand engagements.

GATE 03 SIGNED BLUEPRINT

Owned Part 5 & 6 to Signature

Owned Part 5 Advisory and Part 6 Offer on one full client Blueprint through to the owner's executed physical signature.

GATE 04 OBSERVED HARD TALK

Led 1 Observed Hard Truth

Led a live client discussion and successfully delivered at least one uncomfortable truth under pressure, observed and signed off by the Lead Senior.

GATE 05 PEER COACHING

Coached Another Consultant

Mentored another consultant through one Blueprint section to a passing Template B4 check without doing the work for them.
