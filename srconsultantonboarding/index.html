DISCOVERY 04 TWO PARADIGMS

Traditional Consultant vs. NF Franchise Architect

Why Franchise Systems Fail
Engagement Moment The Traditional Consultant The NF Franchise Architect
Starting Point Starts from the owner's deck, menu features, and growth targets ("reach 50 branches in 3 years"). Starts from the real daily lives of the end diner and future franchisee in their exact words.
The Owner's Numbers Copies sales, ROI, and payback figures into the package as given by management. Marks every figure Heard, Inferred, Client-said, or Confirmed. Rebuilds strictly on Confirmed register data.
Recurring Problems Fixes the symptom at the branch that complained (e.g. ad-hoc discount, local marketing push). Draws the causal chain until it loops back, and destroys the link that keeps the cycle repeating.
Strategic Advice A long laundry list of twenty recommendations where everything is equal priority. Power Law: One or two critical priorities and an explicit "not yet" list signed by the owner.
Uncomfortable Truths Softened to keep the client happy and protect monthly retainers. Radical Candor: 3 moves (State the weak point, say why it hurts, offer a viable next step).
Meeting Conclusion Hands over a 60-page PDF report. "Next steps: TBD." Relentless Execution: leaves One Next Proof, One Named Owner, One Hard Date.
