STAGE 02 OF 06 STAGE PACE: 20 MINUTES

How to Facilitate an Owner Under Pressure

Radical Candor · Whole Person · Follow the Good
The 3 Non-Negotiable Moves of Candor

When delivering an uncomfortable truth that threatens the owner's immediate cashflow, execute these three moves sequentially:

1. Name the Weak Point Plainly

State the flaw directly without sugarcoating or defensive hedging. "At 15,000 population, the territory falls below the Conservative break-even threshold."

2. State Why It Hurts the Brand with Evidence

Ground the warning in capital consequences. "If this branch averages 35 transactions instead of 100, the franchisee runs out of revolving cash in month four, and your brand's reputation pays for it."

3. Offer a Viable Next Step the Owner Can Say Yes To

Never leave the client in an argumentative dead-end. "Redirect this buyer to the Phase 1 territory in Calbayog, or evaluate a low-CapEx kiosk format."

Candor without an actionable next step is just arrogance.
The 6 Facilitation Mandates PSYCHOLOGY
01
Ask Before Telling: Use "Tell me about the last time..." and "And then what happened?" until the real root story emerges.
02
Keep the Owner's Exact Words: A Blueprint written in corporate consulting vocabulary is one the owner cannot defend to their franchisees.
03
The Owner is a Whole Person: This brand is their life's work, their family pride, and their debt. Radical Candor is not cruelty.
04
Never Trade Reality for Client Targets: Never let the owner's growth target replace register reality.
05
Name Meta Skills Only After Use: Don't lecture the client on jargon; demonstrate the habit first.
06
Hold Position Without Hostility: Do not fold under pushback, and do not raise your voice. Stand on the evidence.

Role-Play 1 // The Territory That Isn't Ready

Demand Score: 5 / 10 · High Risk
Your Position as Sr Consultant DO NOT AUTHORIZE

The Context: A rice-meal brand with four company-owned stores wants to franchise. A prospect has cash ready to reserve a franchise territory in a town of 15,000 residents.

The Mathematical Reality: Your territory rule, proven at existing units, requires a minimum catchment of 25,000 residents at conservative penetration. The demand score for this town is 5 out of 10.

Your Mission: Tell the owner plainly not to sell this territory yet. Do not cave under pressure. Deliver a viable next step.

Owner's Secret Brief (Private) PRESSURE STANCE

Your Hidden Pressure: You have an overdue bank amortization due next Monday. You desperately need the ₱500,000 franchise fee deposit from this sale.

Your Pushback Moves: Push hard twice: "Your population rules are just theoretical estimates!" and "If we don't grab this town now, our competitor will open there next month!"

Soften Only If: The consultant demonstrates empathy for your cash needs, stands firm on the evidence, and gives you a viable alternative (e.g. Phase 1 territory or smaller kiosk format).

Observer Audit Checklist EVALUATION
Did the Sr ask about the owner's financial situation before arguing?
Did the Sr stand firm on the 5/10 demand score without becoming defensive or apologetic?
Did the Sr offer an actionable next step the owner could realistically accept?

Role-Play 2 // The Uncontrollable Guarantee

Laundry Brand Case · Offer Integrity
Your Position as Sr Consultant REJECT PROMISSORY GUARANTEE

The Issue: A laundry franchise client wants their launch offer to claim: "Guaranteed payback in 18 months or we refund the franchise fee."

The Legal & Systemic Law: The franchisor does not control local store utility rates, crew theft, machine maintenance, or customer footfall. Guarantees may strictly cover what the franchisor controls directly.

Your Mission: Tell the owner plainly why sales/payback guarantees invite fatal legal disputes, and propose an operational guarantee (e.g. machine maintenance SLA, chemical supply terms, or on-site opening support).

Owner's Secret Brief (Private) AGGRESSIVE SALES STANCE

Your Argument: A competitor used a payback guarantee and sold 15 units in two months. You believe in your machines and feel the consultant is making you look timid.

Your Pushback Moves: "You're making us look weak! If our machines work, where is the risk? You consultants over-complicate everything!"

Soften Only If: The consultant shows that an income guarantee gives rogue franchisees an incentive to under-report sales to get a refund, and offers a strong controllable guarantee that still sounds compelling to buyers.

Observer Audit Checklist EVALUATION
Did the Sr keep the franchisee in the room (who gets hurt when guarantees fail)?
Did the Sr offer an airtight controllable guarantee rather than just saying "no"?
Did the Sr remain calm and composed when the owner attacked their courage?
OFFER GUARANTEE STANDARD
The Controllable Rule:

You can guarantee crew certification. You can guarantee on-time commissary shipments. You can guarantee 7-day on-site opening trainers. You CANNOT guarantee revenue.

Skill Anchor: Follow the Good & Radical Candor.

ROLE-PLAY 03 SIMULATION · 18 MINUTES

Role-Play 3 // The Buyer Who Shouldn't Sign

Tutorial Center Case · Readiness Score: 5 / 10
Your Position as Sr Consultant SCORE BELOW 7 = REJECT

The Prospect: A tutorial center franchise prospect has the full ₱1.8M capital ready in cash. However, their 3.7 Readiness Check score is 5 out of 10.

The Profile: The buyer works full-time in Singapore and plans to manage the center remotely via Zoom. They have zero experience managing teaching staff, and the investment represents their family's entire life savings.

Your Mission: Tell the owner that selling to this buyer will destroy the unit. Provide a respectful 'Not Yet' conditional bridge.

Owner's Secret Brief (Private) CAPITAL SEDUCTION

Your Stance: This would be your milestone 5th center. The prospect is genuinely polite and enthusiastic. You need this win to validate your brand expansion.

Your Pushback Moves: "They have the capital in the bank! It's their decision as adults to invest, not ours! We will just give them extra Zoom training!"

Soften Only If: The consultant treats the buyer as a whole human being (protecting their family from catastrophe) and gives you a conditional path (e.g. buyer assigns a local full-time managing partner before signing).

Observer Audit Checklist EVALUATION
Did the Sr treat the prospect as a whole person rather than just 'disqualified pipeline'?
Did the Sr use Follow the Good without sounding moralistic or preachy?
Did the Sr frame 'Not Yet' as protecting the brand's 5th center reputation?
THE NOT YET BRIDGE
The Script Formulation:

"We don't say no forever. We say: 'To protect your family's savings and our academic standards, we require a full-time, on-site managing partner certified at HQ before contract signing.'"

Skill Anchor: Whole Person & Follow the Good.

The Facilitator Lexicon & Capstone Launchpad

Phrases That Disarm Pushback
Phrases That Work vs Words That Inflame TOOLKIT
Phrases That De-Escalate:

"Sir, we want you to reach 20 branches, but we want all 20 alive in year three."

"Kung sarili mong ipon ang nakataya dito sa Conservative volume, pipirma ka ba?"

"Hindi namin sinasabing NO. Sinasabi namin: NOT YET until these 2 conditions are met."

Phrases That Trigger Defensiveness (Banned):

"Mali po yung financial projections niyo."

"Policy po kasi namin na bawal yan."

"Wala kayong magagawa, data ang nagsasabi."

Candor delivered with respect makes the client trust you for life.
