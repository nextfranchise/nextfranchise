THE DESTINATION GATE

Where Does the Card Land?

Every accepted Problem Card is converted into a concrete section of the Blueprint. It never floats in vacuum:

5.1 Foundation Upgrade: If it changes a term, site cap, or unit economics model.
5.2 Risk & Mitigation: If it identifies an operational breakdown that requires a buffer or audit standard.
Appendix B Open Item: If it requires Legal Counsel redrafting or owner confirmation before launch.
Gate Requirement: 3 Problem Cards accepted across 2+ brands.

STEP 01 (5 MINS) VOICE

Customer's Voice

"I am the franchisee and..."

No solutions permitted in the sentence! Strictly state their daily friction and fear. Never say "The client wants..."

Skill: Customer Obsession
STEP 02 (10 MINS) AUDIT

Assumptions Table

1. [CS] Client-Said
2. [I] Inferred
3. [H] Heard
4. [C] Confirmed

List at least 8 assumptions. Trap: Treating Client-Said as Confirmed. Rebuild on Confirmed data only.

Skill: First Principles
STEP 03 (15 MINS) LOOP

The Causal Chain

A → B → C → D → Loops back to A.

Draw the loop until it repeats. Show why fixing symptoms leaves the underlying systemic driver intact.

Skill: Systems Thinking
STEP 04 (5 MINS) FOCUS

The ONE Link

Attack: Link B only.
Not Yet: Everything else.

Cut to one single priority that moves 80% of the outcome. Everything else is pushed to the owner-approved "not yet" list.

Skill: Power Law

DISCOVERY 05 DEFENSE CLINIC · 35 MINUTES

Defending the Card // The Sr Review Questions

Peer Challenge Protocol
The Senior Reviewer's Challenge Arsenal 5 MIN PER SPRINT

When a trainee presents their Problem Card, the reviewer challenges the logic using these exact questions:

1. The Voice Challenge:

"Whose words are these? Did you hear them directly from a franchisee, or are you echoing the owner's deck?"

2. The Causal Loop Challenge:

"Why does this keep happening? If we fix this one link, why won't the problem just pop up somewhere else?"

3. The Power Law Challenge:

"If you could fix only ONE thing this quarter, is this honestly the single link that moves 80% of the outcome?"

4. The Personal Savings Challenge:

"Would you put your own family's life savings into this unit under these assumptions?"

Any "no" triggers an immediate revision before client presentation.
Cohort Decision // Card Triaging Gate ACCEPT OR REVISE

After the 5-minute challenge, the room votes on one of three triage destinations:

A
Accepted into 5.1 Foundation Upgrades:

Changes a contract clause, territory population threshold, or fee formula.

B
Accepted into 5.2 Named Risks:

A known operational vulnerability with an owned mitigation protocol.

C
Revising: Needs Ground Evidence First:

Card blocked until consultant visits store or conducts customer interview.

Accepted cards become verified evidence for Gate Item 2.
