Card 4.2 Fair Share // Testing Fee Burdens at Break-Even

Anyone can design fees that work when a store is booming at Target. A Senior Consultant proves how the fee structure lands when a store is struggling at break-even.

Snaxxville: Fixed ₱30,000 / mo (No Royalty) FIXED FEE

Represents 3.7% of sales at Target, but jumps to 9.6% of sales at break-even. Design Choice: System fee negotiated or subsidized during soft opening.

Teamworks: ₱100 Per Therapy Session PER-UNIT FEE

Represents exactly 7.4% of sales at every level. Design Choice: The first 100 client sessions per month are waived so fixed clinic overhead is covered first.

The White Bucket: 5% Royalty + 2% Marketing HIGH-RISK ROYALTY

At break-even (66 covers a day), the 7% franchisor deduction consumes 100% of the franchisee's profit! A slow opening is fatal.

"5% royalty is industry standard" is a lazy answer. Standard for whom, and at what volume?

Template B8: The 5 Tests of Offer Legitimacy ALL 5 MUST PASS
1. Guarantees: Only What the Franchisor Controls PASS

DISCOVERY 06 CLINIC LAB · 03:05 – 04:00 (55 MINS)

The Card Clinic // Triads & Quality Check Protocol

Peer Auditing · Radical Candor in Action
The 7-Minute Triad Audit Protocol TRIADS OF 3

Each trainee brings 2 cards from their live client: the one they are proudest of, and the one they are least sure about.

Minute 1 to 2: Author reads card aloud verbatim.
Minute 3 to 5: Reviewers run the card's specific quality check from the manual and deliver one unflinching "What's Weak Here" line.
Minute 6: Author responds only with: "What I will change is..."
Minute 7 (The Output): Every card ends in 1 of 3 outcomes: Rewrite immediately, Visit the ground source, or Move claim to Appendix B.
When a card fails its check, send it back to its source, not to rewording.
Updating the Blueprint Post-Approval GOVERNANCE
Two Distinct Modification Protocols:

1. Update Blueprint: Used only when Foundation cards (Parts 1 to 5) require revision based on newly confirmed register data or store closures. Requires formal version bump.

2. Build Offer: Used to launch new seasonal or founding batches (Part 6) without altering the signed operating foundation.
